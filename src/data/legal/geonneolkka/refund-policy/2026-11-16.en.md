This policy sets out how payments for "Geonneolkka Pro" ("Pro"), the paid product of the Geonneolkka app (the "App") provided by Leafworks (the "Company"), can be cancelled and refunded. Anything not covered here follows the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/geonneolkka/terms-of-service/) and applicable law.

## 1. The product

- Pro is a lifetime pass: you pay once and keep using it. It is digital content whose features unlock as soon as the payment is complete.
- The features included in Pro, its price, and the fact that cancellation may be limited after payment are shown on the Pro screen in the App before you pay.
- Before paying, you can try part of Pro's features for free (3 favorites, 5 place searches a day, one next-green time, and so on).

## 2. How to get a refund, by where you paid

Pro is paid for only through the App Store or Google Play, and the Company never receives payment details such as card numbers. Refunds therefore go through the store you paid with.

| Where you paid | How to get a refund |
|---|---|
| App Store (iPhone, iPad) | Apple decides on and processes refunds. The Company cannot refund App Store payments directly. Choose the Pro purchase at [Apple Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and request a refund. |
| Google Play (Android) | Within 48 hours of payment you can request a refund yourself from your order history in Google Play; Google decides on and processes it. In the defect and minor cases in section 3, at any time, send your Google Play order number (starting with GPA) to [support@leafworks.dev](mailto:support@leafworks.dev), and the Company will check it and issue the refund. |

If an App Store payment must be refunded in the defect or minor cases in section 3 or at the end of the service in section 6, and Apple does not refund it, the Company will return the amount you paid by bank transfer. The bank details are used only for the refund and kept as described in the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/geonneolkka/privacy-policy/).

## 3. When you can get a refund

- **Change of mind**: App Store refunds are decided by Apple, and Google Play refunds requested in Google Play within 48 hours of payment are decided by Google. Otherwise, because Pro is digital content whose features unlock right after payment, withdrawal is limited under Article 17(2)5 of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. Under Article 17(6) of the same Act, the Company shows this on the Pro screen before you pay and lets you try part of Pro for free, as in section 1.
- **If Pro differs from its description or does not work properly**: you can request a refund within 3 months of payment, or within 30 days of finding out. If the Company can fix the problem so that you can use Pro properly, we will offer that first.
- **If a minor paid without a legal guardian's consent**: the legal guardian can cancel the payment. Please ask the store you paid through first; if it is not handled, tell the Company and it will refund as described in section 2 as far as it can verify.
- If applicable law gives you a right more favorable than the rules above, that law applies.

## 4. When a refund is not possible

- Pro received for free with a promo code or offer code, because nothing was paid.
- When the same purchase has already been refunded.

## 5. After a refund

- Once refunded, Pro is removed from that store account, and the App returns to the free features the next time it checks your purchases with the store.
- Information on your device, such as favorites, is not deleted. However, you cannot add more favorites beyond the free limit (3), and Pro features such as departure alerts can no longer be used.

## 6. End of the service

If the service ends under the Terms of Service, we will announce it in the App and on this page 30 days in advance and stop selling Pro from then. If less than 3 months have passed between your Pro payment and the day we announce the end, we will refund the amount you paid on request.

## 7. Contact

- Email: [support@leafworks.dev](mailto:support@leafworks.dev)
- When asking about a refund, please include the store you paid through, the payment date, and the order number (starting with GPA for Google Play, or the order ID on the App Store receipt).

This policy applies from November 16, 2026. If it changes, we will announce it in the App and on this page before the change takes effect. Earlier versions remain available from the Versions menu at the top of this page.
