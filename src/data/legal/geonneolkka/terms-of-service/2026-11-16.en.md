## 1. The service

The App processes public data (from the Korean National Police Agency, the Urban Traffic Information Center, the Seoul Metropolitan Government, and others) to estimate the time left on pedestrian signals at intersections and when the next green light comes. You can use it without signing up.

## 3. Nature of the signal information

- The time left and signal times shown in the App are estimates based on public data and are for reference only.
- They may differ from the actual signals because of construction, signal changes due to traffic, delays in data updates, and similar causes.
- Always check the actual traffic light before crossing. Do not cross based on the App alone.
- Signals that change with traffic have no predictable time left, so they are shown only as a "variable signal".
- The Labs feature "Show times for variable signals" estimates unchecked times from the signals checked before and after, so it is more likely than other numbers to differ from the actual signal. It is shown only if you turn it on, as gray numbers marked "Estimate".
