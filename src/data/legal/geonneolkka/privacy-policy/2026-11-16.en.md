*This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.*

Leafworks (the "Company") handles the personal information of people who use the Geonneolkka app (the "App") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. The App has no sign-up and does not ask for your name or phone number. We receive an email address only when you type one in to get a reply to an inquiry and agree to its collection.

## 1. Information we process and why

| Information | When | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Device verification data**<br>Android: a hash of a random installation ID created by the App<br>iOS: the App Attest key ID and public key, and the attestation receipt issued by Apple | When the App first downloads signal data, and every 30 days after that | To block tampered apps and automated abuse | iOS: 90 days after last use<br>Android: not stored separately (deleted with the usage records after 7 days) |
| **Usage records**<br>Number of requests per device verification value per day | When the App downloads signal data | To apply a daily request limit per device | Deleted after 7 days |
| **Signal reports**<br>Intersection and crosswalk, signal timing details, the description you write, app version, device model, device verification value | When you send a signal report | To improve the accuracy of signal information | 1 year from receipt |
| **Signal requests**<br>Coordinates of the spots you pick on the map (one spot for an intersection, both ends for a crosswalk), the request type, the description you write, app version, device model, device verification value | When you ask us to add signal information | To decide which areas and intersections to cover | 1 year from receipt |
| **Inquiries and app bug reports**<br>What you write, app version, device model, device verification value<br>(Optional) an email address for our reply | When you send an inquiry or an app bug report | Answering inquiries and fixing bugs | 1 year from receipt |
| **Crash records and usage statistics**<br>Records of crashes and errors (where the error happened, device model, OS version, app version, time), a random identifier created for each installation, automatically collected usage events such as first open, time in the app, app updates, and in-app purchases, and the country and region estimated from the connecting IP address | While you use a released version of the App | Fixing errors and improving the service | Crash records: 90 days<br>Usage statistics: 2 months (statistics combined into totals may remain in a form that cannot identify anyone) |
| **Access logs**<br>IP address, request time, and similar | When the App sends a request to our server | Running the service and responding to outages | Up to 7 days |

Device verdicts from Google Play Integrity and Apple App Attest are used only for the check and are not stored. The coordinates in a signal request are the spots you pick on the map; your current location is not sent. We receive an email address only if you agree to its collection and use on the inquiry screen. You can send an inquiry without an email address.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to our server. It is deleted when you delete the App.

- **Location**: used to find nearby crosswalks and show where you are on the map. If you turn on Pro's "Nearby arrival alert", it is also used to check, even when you are not using the App, whether you have come near that favorite. In every case your location is used only on the device and is never sent to our server.
- **Favorites, departure and nearby arrival alert settings, display settings, Labs settings, and whether you have bought Pro**

Backups you create with Pro's "Data backup / restore" contain your favorites (the locations and names of crosswalks you use often), departure alerts, and display settings.

- **Save to a file**: saved where you choose (such as the Files app or a cloud storage service) and never sent to our server. The file stays after you delete the App, so delete it yourself if you no longer need it.
- **Back up to iCloud (iOS)**: saved in the iCloud of your Apple account. It does not pass through our server and we cannot see it. You can restore it on devices signed in to the same Apple account, and delete it from iCloud storage management in iOS Settings.

## 3. App permissions

| Permission | Required | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Location (while using the App) | Optional | Finding nearby crosswalks and showing where you are on the map. |
| Location (always) | Optional | Asked only when you turn on a nearby arrival alert. Checks whether you have come near that favorite even when you are not using the App. |
| Notifications | Optional | Sending departure alerts and nearby arrival alerts. |
| Exact alarms (Android) | Optional | Sending departure alerts at the time you set. |

Your location is used only on the device and is never sent to our server. You can use the App without allowing location. It then shows crosswalks around the center of the map. You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except when the law requires it.

## 5. Processing on our behalf and transfer abroad

| Recipient | Country | Items | Purpose | When and how | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. | United States | All information in the table in section 1 except "Crash records and usage statistics" | Running our servers and storage | Sent over the network while you use the App | Same as section 1 |
| Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase) | United States | "Crash records and usage statistics" in section 1 | Collecting and analyzing crash records and usage statistics | Sent over the network while you use a released version of the App | Same as section 1 |

- Cloudflare's privacy contact is [privacyquestions@cloudflare.com](mailto:privacyquestions@cloudflare.com). This transfer is needed for the App to download signal data. If you refuse it, the App cannot download signal data.
- Google's privacy contact is the [Google privacy contact form](https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form). If you turn off Settings › "Send usage statistics and crash reports" in the App, nothing more is sent to Google, the records and identifier left on the device are deleted, and every feature of the App keeps working.

## 6. Information handled directly by other services

- **Kakao Map**: To draw the map, the App downloads map data for the area you are viewing from Kakao. Kakao may process device and connection information while doing so, under Kakao's own privacy policy.
- **Kakao Local (place search)**: When you search for a place, the search words you type are sent to Kakao through our server to get the results. Your location is not sent. The search words stay in our server's operation logs for up to 7 days and are then deleted automatically; apart from that, we do not store the search words or the results. Intersection search happens only on your device.
- **Apple and Google**: Payments (App Store, Google Play), device integrity checks (App Attest, Play Integrity), and iCloud backups are handled by each company. We never receive payment details such as card numbers.

The App shows no ads and does not collect advertising identifiers (ADID, IDFA) or behavioral data for targeted advertising. Usage statistics are collected only through Google Analytics for Firebase as described in section 5 and are not used for advertising.

## 7. Automatic collection tools

The App does not use cookies. To collect crash records and usage statistics, Firebase creates a random identifier for each installation and stores it on the device. It cannot reveal who you are, such as your name or contact details, and it is deleted when you delete the App. If you turn off Settings › "Send usage statistics and crash reports" in the App, nothing more is sent and the identifier is deleted.

Values the App remembers, such as display settings and favorites, are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

## 8. How information is destroyed

- The server deletes information past its retention period automatically on a regular schedule.
- Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.
- We keep no personal information on paper.

## 9. Your rights and how to use them

- You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information by writing to [support@leafworks.dev](mailto:support@leafworks.dev).
- A legal representative or someone you have authorized can also make the request. In that case, please send a letter of authorization with it.
- We will tell you the result within 10 days of receiving the request.
- Because we do not collect account information, it is hard to identify a specific person from our server records. Tell us what you are asking for, and we will handle it as far as we can verify. If you left an email address with an inquiry, we verify with that address.
- Information stored on your device is erased as soon as you delete the App.

## 10. Children under 14

The App is not directed at children under 14. It does not check ages or collect information about children separately. If a legal guardian asks us to delete a child's information, we will delete it without delay as far as we can verify.

## 11. Security measures

- All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Access to server records is limited to the operator.
- Device verification tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.
- Descriptions in reports and inquiries, email addresses, and requested coordinates are not put in the operator's notification messages; they are viewed only on the server.
