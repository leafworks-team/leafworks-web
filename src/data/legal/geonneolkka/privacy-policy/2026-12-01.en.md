*This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.*

Leafworks (the "Company") handles the personal information of people who use the Geonneolkka app (the "App") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. The App has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, phone number, or email address.

## 1. Information we process and why

| Information | When | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Device verification data**<br>Android: a hash of a random installation ID created by the App<br>iOS: the App Attest key ID and public key, and the attestation receipt issued by Apple | When the App first downloads signal data, and every 30 days after that | To block tampered apps and automated abuse | iOS: 90 days after last use<br>Android: not stored separately (deleted with the usage records after 7 days) |
| **Usage records**<br>Number of requests per device verification value per day | When the App downloads signal data | To apply a daily request limit per device | Deleted after 7 days |
| **Reports**<br>Intersection and crosswalk, signal timing details, the description you write, app version, device model, device verification value | When you send a signal report | To improve the accuracy of signal information | 1 year from receipt |
| **Access logs**<br>IP address, request time, and similar | When the App sends a request to our server | Running the service and responding to outages | Up to 7 days |

Device verdicts from Google Play Integrity and Apple App Attest are used only for the check and are not stored.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to our server. It is deleted when you delete the App.

- **Location**: used only to find nearby crosswalks and show where you are on the map.
- **Favorites, display settings, and whether you have bought Pro**

Backup files you create with Pro's "Data backup / restore" contain your favorites (the locations and names of crosswalks you use often) and display settings. They are saved where you choose (such as the Files app or a cloud storage service) and are never sent to our server. They stay after you delete the App, so delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

## 3. App permissions

| Permission | Required | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Optional | Finding nearby crosswalks and showing where you are on the map. Your location is used only on the device and is never sent to our server. |

You can use the App without allowing location. It then shows crosswalks around the center of the map. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except when the law requires it.

## 5. Processing on our behalf and transfer abroad

| Recipient | Country | Items | Purpose | When and how | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. | United States | All information in the table in section 1 | Running our servers and storage | Sent over the network while you use the App | Same as section 1 |

Cloudflare's privacy contact is [privacyquestions@cloudflare.com](mailto:privacyquestions@cloudflare.com). This transfer is needed for the App to download signal data. If you refuse it, the App cannot download signal data.

## 6. Information handled directly by other services

- **Kakao Map**: To draw the map, the App downloads map data for the area you are viewing from Kakao. Kakao may process device and connection information while doing so, under Kakao's own privacy policy.
- **Kakao Local (place search)**: When you search for a place, the search words you type are sent to Kakao through our server to get the results. Your location is not sent. The search words stay in our server's operation logs for up to 7 days and are then deleted automatically; apart from that, we do not store the search words or the results. Intersection search happens only on your device.
- **Apple and Google**: Payments (App Store, Google Play) and device integrity checks (App Attest, Play Integrity) are handled by each company. We never receive payment details such as card numbers.

The App uses no advertising or analytics tools, and does not collect advertising identifiers (ADID, IDFA) or behavioral data for targeted advertising.

## 7. Automatic collection tools

The App does not use cookies or similar automatic collection tools. Values the App remembers, such as display settings and favorites, are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

## 8. How information is destroyed

- The server deletes information past its retention period automatically on a regular schedule.
- Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.
- We keep no personal information on paper.

## 9. Your rights and how to use them

- You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information by writing to [support@leafworks.dev](mailto:support@leafworks.dev).
- A legal representative or someone you have authorized can also make the request. In that case, please send a letter of authorization with it.
- We will tell you the result within 10 days of receiving the request.
- Because we do not collect account information, it is hard to identify a specific person from our server records. Tell us what you are asking for, and we will handle it as far as we can verify.
- Information stored on your device is erased as soon as you delete the App.

## 10. Children under 14

The App is not directed at children under 14. It does not check ages or collect information about children separately. If a legal guardian asks us to delete a child's information, we will delete it without delay as far as we can verify.

## 11. Security measures

- All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Access to server records is limited to the operator.
- Device verification tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.
