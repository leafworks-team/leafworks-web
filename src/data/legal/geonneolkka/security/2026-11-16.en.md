If you find a security vulnerability in the Geonneolkka app or its server, please let us know. To protect our users, please tell the Company before disclosing it publicly.

## How to report

Send your report to [security@leafworks.dev](mailto:security@leafworks.dev).

Including the following helps us check it quickly.

- Where the vulnerability is (app version and platform, or URL)
- How to reproduce it
- The expected impact

## Scope

In scope:

- The Geonneolkka app (iOS, Android)
- The Geonneolkka server (geonneolkka-api.leafworks.dev)

Out of scope:

- Denial of service (DoS) attacks or high-volume request testing
- Social engineering, phishing, and physical attacks
- Vulnerabilities in third-party services themselves, such as Kakao, Apple, Google, or Cloudflare (please report them to each company)
- Systems of public data providers

## What we ask of you

- Test only as much as needed to confirm the issue.
- Do not access or change other users' information. If you see it by accident, stop right away and tell us.
- Do not disrupt the service.
- Do not disclose the issue publicly before it is fixed. If a fix takes a long time, we will agree on a disclosure date together.

## What we will do

- We will confirm receipt within 7 days of your report.
- We will tell you what we found and when we plan to fix it.
- We will not take legal action against good-faith reporters who follow the rules above.
- If you wish, we will add your name to the acknowledgments on this page after the fix.
- We do not offer bounties at this time.
