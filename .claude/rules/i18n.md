# Languages

Korean at `/`, English at `/en/`, Japanese at `/ja/`.

- Every user-facing string lives in `src/i18n/` (`content.ts`, `brand-guide.ts`) or in the
  localized fields of `src/data/apps.ts`. The Korean dictionary defines the shape; a missing
  translation must fail `astro check`, not ship.
- Keep `src/i18n/config.ts` and the `i18n.locales` list in `astro.config.mjs` in step.
- The site font is **Pretendard JP** because of the Japanese locale — plain Pretendard has
  no kanji, so the face would change mid-sentence.
- `word-break: keep-all` is for Korean only; Japanese pages use `line-break: strict`.
- The horizontal tagline stays in English on every locale.
